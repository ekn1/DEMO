The cognitive logistics intelligence layer for emerging markets

SCENTS turns raw movement, signals, and relationships into spatial-cognitive decisions. WISMO turns those decisions into a transparent, real-time experience for every merchant, rider, and customer. One stack — from ingest to insight to doorstep.

Module 01

SCENTS — Spatial-Cognitive Enterprise Tracking

An end-to-end intelligence engine: ingest anything, transform it, reason over it with an autonomous agent loop, and resolve entities with record-linkage. Built for the messiness of real-world, multi-source data.

Module 02

WISMO — Where Is My Order, end to end

A complete order-to-delivery operating system: a gateway, core services for orders/geo/route, branded experiences, WhatsApp-first notifications, and a privacy-safe tracking pixel — with a customer portal for merchants, riders, and clients.

How it fits together

One pipeline, two outcomes

SCENTS produces the intelligence; WISMO delivers and proves it. The same event stream powers back-office cognition and front-office transparency.
